## The Problem with Generic PM Frameworks

Most PM frameworks were built for consumer software: fast feedback loops, cheap experiments, user behavior you can measure directly. Enterprise software is different. Buying cycles are long, stakeholders are numerous, and building the wrong thing is expensive in ways that don't show up until it's too late. The frameworks that work at scale aren't the ones that optimize for speed — they're the ones that enforce rigor at the right decision points.

These tools reflect a specific point of view on how enterprise product work should be structured: stage-gate discipline for initiative management, methodology-grounded coaching for ICs working through ambiguous problems, and pressure-testing for leaders making decisions that ICs can't make alone.

## SDLC System

The SDLC System is a stage-gate product development framework that runs as a Cowork plugin. It manages initiatives from opportunity discovery through post-launch signal reporting — each phase producing a specific artifact, each gate a real decision point rather than a rubber stamp.

Phases run from discovery (problem validation, opportunity briefs) through scoping (product briefs, PRDs, engineering handoff) into launch and post-launch operating reviews. At each gate, the system checks artifact completeness and produces a decision-ready brief for product leadership. Signal reports after launch track whether the initiative is performing as designed.

The goal is not process for its own sake. It's a system that forces the right conversations to happen at the right time, with the right documentation, before teams are too committed to course-correct.

## Product IC Coach

Product IC Coach is a coaching agent built for individual contributors working through the hard parts of PM craft: defining problems that are worth solving, assessing evidence quality honestly, shaping rough ideas before they harden, and positioning a recommendation so it can survive scrutiny.

The coaching runs in two phases. Phase 1 is Socratic — drawing out the PM's position before any challenge is made. Phase 2 steelmans the opposition: the strongest case against the current direction. The session lands with a forced synthesis and a recommended position the PM has to respond to.

Specialized skills handle the areas where ICs most often get into trouble: customer understanding (who you're actually building for, not who you imagine), problem statement sharpening, evidence audits (grading signal quality honestly), idea shaping at the pre-brief stage, and prioritization under real capacity constraints.

## Product Leadership Coach

Product Leadership Coach runs the same Phase 1 / Phase 2 / Land mechanics, calibrated for the decisions that belong to leaders rather than ICs: org design, team situations, strategic calls, hiring and leveling. The coaching adjusts based on what's at stake — more Socratic groundwork before going adversarial on high-stakes decisions, faster to challenge on tactical ones.
